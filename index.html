AIML-501 · Artifact 01

AI & ML Timelines

From early theories of machine intelligence to today's generative AI, this timeline highlights how technical breakthroughs evolved into tools that reshape work, communication, and business decision-making.

The evolution

Milestones that changed AI

1950

Alan Turing proposes the imitation game

Turing's work introduces a foundational question: can machines exhibit behavior that appears intelligent?

1956

The Dartmouth workshop

Researchers formalize artificial intelligence as a field of study, setting a research agenda for machine intelligence.

1957–1958

The perceptron

Frank Rosenblatt develops an early neural-network model, helping establish a foundation for machine learning research.

1986

Backpropagation gains prominence

Influential research demonstrates practical methods for training multilayer neural networks.

1997

Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov

IBM's chess system demonstrates the power of specialized computation and search in a complex decision domain.

2012

AlexNet accelerates deep learning

A major image-recognition result showcases how neural networks, large datasets, and GPUs can transform computer vision.

2016

AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol

Deep learning and reinforcement learning reach a widely recognized milestone in the game of Go.

2017

The Transformer architecture

Attention-based models open a new chapter in language modeling and many later generative AI systems.

2022

Conversational generative AI becomes mainstream

The public launch of ChatGPT broadens awareness of natural-language interfaces and generative AI's opportunities and risks.
